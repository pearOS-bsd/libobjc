# Provenance — what was taken from other projects

Everything under `upstream/` is unmodified source of other open projects, copied for study and as the starting point of our implementation.  Each folder keeps the licence files it came with.  Our own changes are made outside `upstream/` (or marked in the file).  Sources only: binary files and files over 1 MB were left out.
